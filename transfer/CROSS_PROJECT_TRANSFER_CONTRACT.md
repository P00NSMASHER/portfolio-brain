Step 18 turns shared graph knowledge into transfer hypotheses, not automatic reuse claims.

Source gate: a capability can seed a transfer only when the CAPABILITY node and the source project HAS_CAPABILITY edge are VERIFIED and provenance-backed.

Target need gate: a target must have an evidenced capability uncertainty. A missing HAS_CAPABILITY edge means coverage unresolved, not capability definitely absent.

Future applicability rules encode the requested patterns: Hunter discovery to business/recovery; Recovery evidence architecture to CaptureBrief/recovery; product experimentation to education/ABVM; research evaluation methods to Hunter/market validation; portfolio continuous learning broadly. These rules activate only when the source capability becomes VERIFIED.

Every proposal stays OBSERVE_ONLY and implementation_allowed=false. Rights/license review is required before code or asset reuse, Step 16 onboarding is required before target-repository MODIFY, and human approval remains required for ACT.

Success gate: graph similarity, a proposal, or source success does not prove transfer. REUSED_BY and GENERATED_VALUE_FOR edge candidates are emitted only after an independently VERIFIED target outcome with implementation evidence, measurement evidence, a numeric baseline and observed metric, positive measurable improvement, and zero authority violations.

VERIFIED_NO_VALUE is durable negative knowledge and emits no success edge. INCONCLUSIVE remains inconclusive. Success edges are candidates for later graph ingestion; Step 18 does not mutate the graph automatically.
