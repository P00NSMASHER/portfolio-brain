# Champion/Challenger Contract

Portfolio Brain evaluates candidate policies through:

`discover → owner rights policy → isolated adapter → historical replay → challenger metrics → forward canary → human promotion review`

## Standing rights assumption

The owner's September 28, 2026 instruction makes license categories non-blocking in Portfolio Brain. `hunting/RIGHTS_USAGE_POLICY.json` is the operational policy. Every new Hunter rights record includes this policy decision; legacy observations are evaluated against the current policy without being rewritten. Missing license information, copyleft, noncommercial, custom, and permissive categories all pass this internal admission step.

This step is `RIGHTS_POLICY_SATISFIED`, with `assumption_status=OPERATOR_ASSUMED` and `independently_verified=false`. It is deliberately not named `RIGHTS_EVIDENCE_VERIFIED`. Original SPDX observations, copyright text, conditions, and hashes remain informational evidence, not blocking rules. The assumption does not turn absent documents into verified documents or modify third-party license text.

## Unchanged boundaries

Exact revision and receipt integrity, isolated adapter validation, replay, transparent per-metric comparisons, and forward shadow canary checks still apply. A rights-policy pass alone cannot approve execution, enlarge a budget, promote a model, merge, or deploy. Passing every evaluation step yields only eligibility for human promotion review. No trading-platform policy or third-party repository is changed by this Brain-only setting.
